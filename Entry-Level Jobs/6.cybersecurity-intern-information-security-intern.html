90-Day Roadmap For Cybersecurity Intern / Information Security Intern

Your structured path to gaining practical experience, understanding enterprise security, and contributing to a cybersecurity team.

Phase 1: Onboarding & Foundational Understanding

Days 1-30: Integrating into the Team and Grasping Core Concepts

🎯 Phase 1 Goals

Company Policies Team Structure Basic Security Concepts IT Infrastructure Overview Documentation Review

Week 1: Onboarding & Introductions

  • Complete HR and IT onboarding, get access to necessary systems and tools.
  • Meet the cybersecurity team and understand their roles and responsibilities.
  • Familiarize yourself with the company's mission, values, and overall business operations.
  • Review company-wide security policies and acceptable use guidelines.
Company Culture Team Dynamics Security Policies Access Management

Task: Create an Onboarding Checklist

Document your onboarding process and create a checklist for future interns, including key contacts and initial setup steps.

Week 2: IT Infrastructure & Networking Basics (Organizational Context)

  • Gain an overview of the organization's network topology and key systems.
  • Understand how common network protocols (DNS, DHCP, HTTP/S) are used within the company.
  • Learn about the company's asset inventory system and how assets are categorized.
  • Shadow IT/Network teams to observe operational procedures.
Network Topology Asset Management Enterprise Networking Shadowing

Task: Map Key IT Assets

Identify and document 5-10 critical IT assets (servers, applications) and their general function within the organization.

Week 3: Core Security Concepts & Tools (Enterprise View)

  • Understand the company's approach to the CIA Triad and risk management.
  • Learn about common threats and vulnerabilities relevant to the organization's industry.
  • Introduction to the company's primary security tools (e.g., EDR, SIEM, vulnerability scanner).
  • Review past security incidents (if permissible and anonymized) to understand response procedures.
Risk Management Enterprise Threats Security Tools Incident Review

Task: Research a Recent Industry Cyber Attack

Prepare a brief summary of a recent cyber attack in the company's industry, outlining its impact and lessons learned.

Week 4: Security Awareness & Basic Operations Support

  • Participate in internal security awareness training sessions.
  • Assist with basic security operations tasks, such as reviewing low-priority alerts.
  • Learn about the ticketing system and how security incidents/requests are managed.
  • Contribute to internal security documentation updates (e.g., updating a policy draft).
Security Awareness Ticketing Systems Basic Alert Triage Documentation

Task: Draft a Security Tip Sheet

Create a short, user-friendly tip sheet on a common security topic (e.g., "How to spot a phishing email") for employees.

🎯 Milestone 1 Achievement

You have successfully onboarded, understood the company's IT and security landscape, familiarized yourself with core security concepts in an enterprise context, and begun contributing to basic security operations and documentation.

Projects After Phase 1 (Intern-Specific)

Apply your initial understanding with these hands-on projects, often in collaboration with team members.

Security Tool Feature Research

Research a specific feature of one of the company's security tools (e.g., a new SIEM correlation rule, EDR capability) and present findings.

Hints: Consult vendor documentation, internal wikis, and team experts.

Internal Security Policy Review

Review a specific internal security policy (e.g., password policy, remote access policy) and suggest minor improvements or clarifications.

Hints: Compare to industry best practices (e.g., NIST guidelines).

Basic Vulnerability Scan Report Assistance

Assist in reviewing a recent vulnerability scan report, helping to categorize findings or identify false positives.

Hints: Learn the company's vulnerability management process and tools.

Security Awareness Campaign Idea

Propose an idea for a new or improved internal security awareness campaign, targeting a specific employee group or threat.

Hints: Focus on engaging content and clear calls to action.

Phase 2: Practical Application & Support

Days 31-60: Deeper Engagement in Security Operations and Initiatives

🎯 Phase 2 Goals

Log Analysis Support Vulnerability Management Support Incident Response Support Security Documentation Compliance Awareness

Week 5: Log Review & SIEM Support

  • Assist in monitoring and triaging security alerts within the SIEM.
  • Learn to navigate and query logs from various sources (e.g., firewall, server, application logs).
  • Understand the lifecycle of a security alert from detection to closure.
  • Participate in daily/weekly security operations meetings.
SIEM (Company Specific) Log Queries Alert Triage Security Ops

Task: Investigate a Low-Priority Alert

Under supervision, investigate a low-priority security alert in the SIEM and document your findings and proposed next steps.

Week 6: Endpoint Security & Patch Management Assistance

  • Understand the company's endpoint protection strategy (EPP/EDR).
  • Assist with basic endpoint security tasks, such as reviewing quarantined files or device health.
  • Learn about the patch management process and assist with tracking patch compliance.
  • Familiarize with asset management and configuration management databases (CMDB).
EPP/EDR Patching CMDB Endpoint Hardening

Task: Research a Recent Vulnerability (CVE)

Select a recent critical CVE and research its impact, affected systems, and potential mitigation strategies relevant to the company's environment.

Week 7: Vulnerability Management & Remediation Support

  • Assist in running scheduled vulnerability scans on internal systems.
  • Help analyze scan results, identify false positives, and categorize vulnerabilities.
  • Learn about the remediation process and assist in tracking remediation efforts.
  • Understand the importance of risk scoring and prioritization of vulnerabilities.
Vulnerability Scanners Risk Scoring Remediation Tracking CVE Analysis

Task: Draft a Remediation Plan for a Low-Risk Vulnerability

Based on a scan report, draft a simple remediation plan for a low-risk vulnerability, including steps and responsible parties.

Week 8: Incident Response & Forensics Awareness

  • Participate in incident response drills or tabletop exercises (if available).
  • Understand the phases of the incident response lifecycle (preparation, detection, containment, eradication, recovery, post-incident).
  • Learn about basic forensic data collection (e.g., disk images, memory dumps) and chain of custody principles.
  • Assist in organizing incident response documentation and playbooks.
Incident Response Tabletop Exercises Digital Forensics Playbooks

Task: Outline an Incident Response Scenario

Choose a simple security incident (e.g., a lost laptop) and outline the key steps the company would take to respond.

🚀 Milestone 2 Achievement

You have actively participated in security operations, gained hands-on experience with log analysis and vulnerability management, and developed an understanding of incident response procedures within an organizational context.

Projects After Phase 2 (Intern-Specific)

These projects will allow you to apply your growing expertise to more focused tasks, often with direct impact.

Automate a Small Security Task (Scripting)

Write a simple script (e.g., Python, PowerShell) to automate a repetitive security task, such as parsing logs or checking a configuration.

Hints: Identify a manual task, get guidance on scripting best practices, focus on small scope.

Cloud Security Best Practices Research

Research and summarize best practices for securing a specific cloud service (e.g., AWS S3, Azure VMs) that the company uses or plans to use.

Hints: Focus on IAM, network security, and data protection in the cloud.

Security Compliance Mapping (Basic)

Pick a small aspect of a compliance framework (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS) and map company practices to its requirements.

Hints: Work with GRC team members, focus on a few specific controls.

Threat Intelligence Briefing

Prepare a brief on an emerging threat (e.g., a new ransomware strain, a specific APT group) and its potential relevance to the organization.

Hints: Use reputable threat intelligence sources, focus on actionable insights.

Phase 3: Project Contribution & Career Exploration

Days 61-90: Driving Your Project and Planning Your Future in Cybersecurity

🎯 Phase 3 Goals

Project Ownership Presentation Skills Professional Networking Career Path Exploration Feedback Integration

Week 9: Deep Dive into Intern Project

  • Focus intensively on your assigned intern project, applying learned skills.
  • Collaborate closely with your mentor and relevant team members.
  • Identify any roadblocks and proactively seek solutions or assistance.
  • Begin outlining the structure and content for your final project presentation.
Project Management Collaboration Problem Solving Planning

Task: Mid-Internship Project Review

Present your project progress to your mentor and team, incorporating their feedback for the final weeks.

Week 10: Cross-Functional Collaboration & Reporting

  • Engage with other departments (e.g., Development, Operations, Legal) to understand their security needs and challenges.
  • Practice communicating technical security concepts to non-technical audiences.
  • Refine your project findings and begin drafting a formal report or documentation.
  • Understand the importance of metrics and reporting in cybersecurity.
Cross-functional Comms Technical Writing Reporting Stakeholder Mgmt

Task: Draft a Project Summary Report

Write a concise summary report of your intern project, highlighting objectives, methodologies, findings, and recommendations.

Week 11: Final Project Preparation & Presentation

  • Finalize your intern project deliverables and documentation.
  • Prepare and rehearse your final presentation for the team and/or management.
  • Gather feedback from your mentor and peers on your presentation style and content.
  • Reflect on your overall internship experience and key learnings.
Presentation Skills Public Speaking Self-Reflection Deliverables

Task: Deliver Final Project Presentation

Present your intern project to the relevant stakeholders, showcasing your work and answering questions.

Week 12: Career Planning & Networking

  • Have a debriefing session with your mentor and manager to discuss your performance and future career goals.
  • Seek advice on potential full-time roles within cybersecurity and necessary skills development.
  • Network with other professionals within the company and in the broader industry.
  • Update your resume and LinkedIn profile with your internship experience and achievements.
Career Development Mentorship Professional Networking Resume/LinkedIn

Task: Exit Interview & Feedback

Participate in an exit interview, providing constructive feedback on the internship program and receiving final performance insights.

🏆 Final Achievement

Congratulations! You have successfully completed your Cybersecurity / Information Security Internship, having integrated into a professional security team, contributed to real-world projects, gained practical experience with enterprise tools and processes, and built a foundation for your cybersecurity career.

Expand on Internship Project

Take your intern project and expand on it in your personal lab, adding more features or depth, to showcase on your resume.

Hints: Document your expanded work on GitHub, write a blog post about it.

Home Lab Security Hardening

Hints: Implement strong access controls, network segmentation, centralized logging.

Practice Interview Scenarios

Contribute to an Open-Source Security Tool

Find a small open-source security project and contribute a bug fix, documentation, or a minor feature.

Hints: Start with projects that have good beginner-friendly issues labeled.

Recommended Resources

  • "Blue Team Handbook: Incident Response Tactics" by Don Murdoch
  • "Cybersecurity for Dummies" (for broad overview)
  • "The Phoenix Project" (for understanding IT operations)

Project Ideas (Internship Focused)

Hands-on projects to solidify your learning and build your professional portfolio during the internship

Basic Projects

  • Internal Wiki Contribution: Create or significantly update a section of the internal security wiki based on your learnings.
  • Tool Feature Comparison: Research and compare 2-3 features of a security tool the company uses, presenting pros and cons.
  • Phishing Email Analysis: Analyze a sanitized phishing email (from a company's spam trap) and document its characteristics.
  • Basic Script for Log Parsing: Write a simple Python/PowerShell script to parse a specific type of log file for certain keywords.
  • Security Best Practices Checklist: Develop a checklist for employees on a specific security topic (e.g., "Secure Browsing").

Intermediate Projects

  • Vulnerability Remediation Tracking Improvement: Propose or implement a small improvement to the company's vulnerability tracking process.
  • Incident Response Playbook Enhancement: Contribute to an existing incident response playbook by adding details for a specific scenario.
  • Cloud Security Configuration Review: Review the security configuration of a non-critical cloud resource (e.g., a test S3 bucket) against best practices.
  • Security Awareness Module Development: Develop a short, interactive security awareness module or presentation on a targeted topic.
  • Threat Intelligence Feed Integration (Conceptual): Research how a new threat intelligence feed could be integrated into the company's SIEM.

Advanced Projects (with significant mentorship)

  • Small Security Automation Script: Develop and implement a script to automate a routine security task, such as user account auditing or certificate expiry checks.
  • Security Control Mapping: Map a subset of the company's security controls to a specific industry framework (e.g., NIST CSF, ISO 27001).
  • Forensic Artifact Collection Guide: Create a detailed guide for collecting specific forensic artifacts from a compromised system.
  • Web Application Security Scan & Report: Conduct a basic scan on an internal test web application and provide a summary of findings.
  • Zero Trust Principle Application: Research a specific component of Zero Trust and propose how it could be applied to a company system.

Portfolio Building Projects (Personal Lab)

  • Build a Mini-SOC Environment: Set up a personal lab with a SIEM (e.g., Wazuh, Splunk Free) and simulate log collection from various sources.
  • Vulnerable Web App Exploitation (Ethical Lab): Practice finding and exploiting vulnerabilities in a controlled vulnerable web application (e.g., DVWA).
  • Linux Server Hardening Script: Write a script to automate the hardening of a Linux server based on industry best practices.
  • Network Traffic Analysis Challenge: Analyze a provided PCAP file (e.g., from a CTF) to identify malicious activity.
  • Simple Password Cracker: Develop a basic password cracking tool in Python for educational purposes (ethical lab use only).

What Next? Leveraging Your Internship for Career Success

Your internship is a stepping stone. Here's how to build on this experience.

1. Reflect & Document

  • Summarize Learnings: Create a detailed log of what you learned, tools you used, and challenges you overcame.
  • Update Resume & LinkedIn: Immediately update your professional profiles with your internship title, responsibilities, and achievements. Quantify your impact where possible.
  • Portfolio Projects: Refine and document your internship projects (if shareable) or create new personal projects based on your learnings for your portfolio.

2. Network & Connect

  • Stay in Touch: Maintain contact with your mentor, manager, and team members. They are valuable references and potential future colleagues.
  • Expand Your Network: Connect with other professionals you met during your internship, both inside and outside the company.
  • Attend Events: Continue participating in local cybersecurity meetups, webinars, and conferences to expand your network.

3. Continue Learning & Certify

  • Targeted Learning: Based on your internship experience, identify areas you enjoyed or need to improve and pursue further learning.
  • Certifications: Pursue entry-level certifications like CompTIA Security+ or vendor-specific certifications that align with your interests.
  • Online Labs: Continue using platforms like TryHackMe and Hack The Box to hone your practical skills.

4. Job Search & Application

  • Tailor Applications: Customize your resume and cover letter for each job application, highlighting relevant internship experiences.
  • Practice Interviews: Prepare for common technical and behavioral interview questions, using your internship experiences as examples.
  • Leverage Referrals: Ask your internship contacts for referrals to open positions within their networks.
