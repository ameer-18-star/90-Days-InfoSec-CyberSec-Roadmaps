90-Day Roadmap For "Security Auditor"

This 90-day roadmap is designed to equip you with the foundational knowledge and practical skills required to excel as a Security Auditor. You'll delve into auditing frameworks, compliance standards, technical assessments, and reporting, preparing you to identify and mitigate cybersecurity risks effectively.

Phase 1: Foundational Knowledge & Frameworks

Days 1-30: Establishing Your Core Understanding

🎯 Phase 1 Goals

Security Principles Risk Management Basics Compliance Frameworks Audit Process Fundamentals Networking & OS Basics

Week 1: Introduction to Information Security & Auditing

  • Understand the CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability).
  • Explore common cyber threats and vulnerabilities.
  • Learn about the role and responsibilities of a Security Auditor.
  • Familiarize yourself with audit methodologies (e.g., ISO 27001, NIST CSF).
  • Study ethical considerations in security auditing.
  • Introduction to basic networking concepts (TCP/IP, common protocols).
  • Understand operating system fundamentals (Windows, Linux basics).
  • Research common security controls (technical, administrative, physical).
ISO 27001 NIST CSF CIA Triad Basic Networking

Task: Define & Document

Create a personal glossary of 20 key information security terms and a brief summary of the ISO 27001 and NIST CSF frameworks, highlighting their main objectives.

Week 2: Risk Management & Controls

  • Delve deeper into risk assessment methodologies (qualitative vs. quantitative).
  • Understand risk treatment strategies (avoid, transfer, mitigate, accept).
  • Explore various types of security controls (preventive, detective, corrective).
  • Learn about security policies, standards, guidelines, and procedures.
  • Analyze a simplified risk register.
  • Understand the basics of access control mechanisms.
  • Research common vulnerability categories (OWASP Top 10 introduction).
  • Study asset classification and data handling policies.
Risk Assessment OWASP Top 10 Security Controls Access Control

Task: Mini Risk Assessment

Conduct a small-scale risk assessment for a hypothetical personal online service (e.g., email or social media), identifying potential threats, vulnerabilities, and existing/missing controls.

Week 3: Compliance & Regulatory Landscape

  • Understand the importance of regulatory compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, SOX, PCI DSS).
  • Examine the key requirements of at least two major compliance standards.
  • Learn about evidence collection and documentation in audits.
  • Familiarize yourself with audit reporting structures and findings.
  • Study the concept of control objectives and control activities.
  • Understand legal and ethical implications of non-compliance.
  • Research the role of internal and external audits.
  • Learn about common audit findings and observations.
GDPR HIPAA PCI DSS SOX

Task: Compliance Checklist Development

Develop a basic compliance checklist for a small business based on a chosen standard (e.g., a simplified GDPR checklist for customer data handling).

Week 4: Technical Fundamentals for Auditors

  • Deep dive into common network security concepts (firewalls, IDS/IPS, VPNs).
  • Understand secure configuration principles for operating systems.
  • Explore basic cryptography and its applications in security.
  • Learn about secure software development lifecycle (SDLC) concepts.
  • Familiarize yourself with common authentication and authorization mechanisms.
  • Understand the role of logging and monitoring in security.
  • Introduction to cloud security concepts (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS security considerations).
  • Basic understanding of database security.
Network Security Cryptography SDLC Cloud Security Basics

Task: Security Control Mapping

Map basic technical security controls (e.g., strong passwords, firewalls) to the CIA Triad principles and explain how they contribute to each.

🎯 Milestone 1 Achievement

You've built a solid understanding of fundamental information security concepts, key audit frameworks, risk management principles, and the regulatory landscape. You can articulate the purpose of security auditing and identify basic security controls.

Projects After Phase 1

These projects will help solidify your foundational understanding through practical application.

Mini Internal Audit Report

Draft a simplified internal audit report for a small, fictional company based on a common control objective (e.g., "Access Control Management"). Include a scope, objective, findings, and recommendations.

Hints: Focus on clear, concise language. Use a structured report format. Even if fictional, make findings realistic based on what you've learned.

Compliance Standard Deep Dive

Choose one compliance standard (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS) and create a presentation (or detailed document) summarizing its core requirements and how an organization would demonstrate compliance.

Hints: Focus on actionable requirements. Think about what an auditor would look for. Use official documentation as your primary source.

Home Network Security Audit (Self-Assessment)

Perform a self-assessment of your home network security. Document your router settings, Wi-Fi security, connected devices, and identify potential vulnerabilities. Propose simple improvements.

Hints: Don't implement changes without understanding the implications. Focus on identifying weak points and proposing logical solutions.

Risk Register Creation

Develop a basic risk register for a small e-commerce website. Identify at least 5 potential risks, assess their likelihood and impact, and propose risk treatment strategies.

Hints: Consider common web application vulnerabilities. Use a simple scoring system for likelihood and impact (e.g., Low, Medium, High).

Phase 2: Technical Auditing & Advanced Standards

Days 31-60: Deepening Technical Acumen and Audit Execution

🎯 Phase 2 Goals

Technical Control Auditing Security Assessment Tools Advanced Compliance Audit Interview Skills Reporting & Remediation

Week 5: Network & System Audit Techniques

  • Learn about auditing network architecture and configurations.
  • Understand how to audit firewalls, routers, and switches for security.
  • Explore techniques for auditing operating system security (Windows Server, Linux).
  • Familiarize yourself with vulnerability scanning concepts and tools.
  • Practice interpreting vulnerability scan results.
  • Understand log review and security information and event management (SIEM) basics.
  • Learn about auditing secure remote access (VPN, RDP).
  • Study best practices for system hardening.
Nessus OpenVAS Wireshark (passive) SIEM Concepts

Task: Vulnerability Scan Analysis

Using a publicly available vulnerability scanner (e.g., a demo version of Nessus or OpenVAS on a test VM), run a scan and analyze the top 5 critical findings, suggesting remediation steps.

Week 6: Application & Database Security Auditing

  • Understand common web application vulnerabilities (OWASP Top 10 in depth).
  • Learn about auditing secure coding practices.
  • Explore techniques for auditing database security (e.g., SQL Server, MySQL).
  • Familiarize yourself with API security auditing concepts.
  • Study authentication and authorization audit points in applications.
  • Learn about input validation and secure session management.
  • Understand the concept of least privilege in database access.
  • Research common misconfigurations in web servers (Apache, Nginx).
OWASP ZAP SQLMap (concepts) Burp Suite (basics) Database Security

Task: Web Application Security Review (Conceptual)

Choose a common web application feature (e.g., user login, file upload) and describe potential vulnerabilities and the audit steps you would take to identify them, referencing OWASP Top 10.

Week 7: Cloud Security Auditing & Identity & Access Management (IAM)

  • Understand cloud service models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS) from an audit perspective.
  • Learn about shared responsibility models in cloud security.
  • Explore auditing practices for major cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP - general concepts).
  • Deep dive into Identity and Access Management (IAM) auditing.
  • Study multi-factor authentication (MFA) and single sign-on (SSO) audit points.
  • Understand privileged access management (PAM) principles and auditing.
  • Research common cloud misconfigurations.
  • Learn about auditing virtual network configurations in the cloud.
AWS Security Hub Azure Security Center IAM CloudTrail/CloudWatch

Task: Cloud Security Policy Review

Draft a high-level cloud security policy for a hypothetical company migrating to AWS/Azure, focusing on key audit areas like access control, data encryption, and logging.

Week 8: Audit Reporting & Communication Skills

  • Master the art of writing clear, concise, and actionable audit reports.
  • Learn how to formulate effective audit findings and recommendations.
  • Develop strong communication skills for interacting with auditees and stakeholders.
  • Practice active listening and effective questioning during audit interviews.
  • Understand follow-up and remediation tracking processes.
  • Learn about presenting audit findings to different audiences.
  • Study the importance of professional skepticism in auditing.
  • Refine your ability to articulate complex technical issues in business terms.
Report Writing Interview Techniques Presentation Skills Remediation Tracking

Task: Mock Audit Interview & Finding Write-up

Pair with a peer (or simulate a scenario) to conduct a mock audit interview. Then, write up a detailed audit finding based on a simulated observation, including root cause and recommendation.

🚀 Milestone 2 Achievement

You can now perform technical security assessments across various domains (network, systems, applications, cloud). You possess the skills to interpret technical data, identify vulnerabilities, and effectively communicate audit findings and recommendations.

Projects After Phase 2

These projects will challenge you to apply your growing technical audit skills and refine your reporting.

Simulated Internal Audit (End-to-End)

Design and execute a simulated internal audit for a specific business process (e.g., employee onboarding or IT asset management). Document scope, objective, procedures, findings, and recommendations in a full report.

Hints: Focus on integrating all learned phases. This is your chance to showcase a holistic audit process.

Cloud Configuration Review

Utilize free tiers or demo environments of cloud providers (AWS, Azure) to explore common security configurations (e.g., S3 bucket policies, IAM roles). Document potential misconfigurations and their impact.

Hints: Never perform unathorized scans or tests on live systems. Use official documentation for guidance on secure configurations.

Security Awareness Training Audit Plan

Develop an audit plan to assess an organization's security awareness training program. What evidence would you look for? What questions would you ask?

Hints: Think about different aspects of training effectiveness: content, delivery, participation, impact on behavior.

Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment (Simulated)

Create a template and conduct a simulated risk assessment for a fictional third-party vendor that processes sensitive data. What questions would you ask? What documents would you request?

Hints: Consider data privacy, security controls, and incident response capabilities of the vendor.

Basic Projects

  • **Home Wi-Fi Security Audit:** Document configurations and identify simple vulnerabilities.
  • **Access Control Matrix Creation:** Create a simple matrix for a small team's access to resources.

Intermediate Projects

  • **Simulated Network Device Configuration Audit:** Review a provided network device configuration file for security weaknesses.
  • **Web Server Hardening Plan:** Propose a hardening plan for a basic web server (e.g., Apache, Nginx).
  • **Cloud Storage Bucket Audit (Conceptual):** Outline the steps to audit a public cloud storage bucket for misconfigurations.
  • **Incident Response Plan Review:** Analyze a sample incident response plan and identify areas for improvement.
  • **User Account Audit Report:** Create a report on a simulated user account audit, highlighting inactive accounts, excessive privileges, etc.

Advanced Projects

  • **Full-Scope Cybersecurity Framework Assessment:** Conduct a simulated assessment of a fictional organization against a framework like NIST CSF or ISO 27001, documenting maturity levels and gaps.
  • **Develop an Automated Audit Script:** Write a Python script to automate a specific audit check (e.g., check for open ports on a range of IPs, parse firewall rules).

  • **Penetration Test Report Analysis:** Analyze a sample penetration test report and translate the technical findings into executive-level audit findings with business impact.
  • **GRC Tool Integration Strategy:** Propose a strategy for integrating a GRC tool into an organization's existing IT environment.

Expert Projects (Ongoing)

  • **Lead a Simulated Audit Team:** Take on a leadership role in a complex simulated audit, delegating tasks and overseeing the entire engagement.
  • **Specialized Compliance Audit:** Deep dive into a niche compliance standard (e.g., CCPA, NERC CIP) and perform a mock audit against it.

  • **Develop Audit Training Material:** Create training modules or presentations for new auditors on specific topics.
  • **Attend & Present at Conferences:** Share your insights and network by presenting a case study or research at a relevant industry conference.

What Next: Beyond 90 Days

Your journey as a Security Auditor is one of continuous learning and adaptation.

1. Continuous Learning & Specialization

  • **Stay Updated:** The threat landscape and compliance requirements constantly evolve. Dedicate time weekly to read industry news, research new vulnerabilities, and understand emerging technologies (AI, blockchain, IoT).
  • **Deepen Expertise:** Consider specializing in areas like cloud security auditing, privacy audits (GDPR, CCPA), application security audits, or operational technology (OT) security audits.
  • **Advanced Certifications:** Pursue more advanced certifications like CISSP, CISM, or cloud-specific security certifications (AWS Certified Security, Azure Security Engineer).

2. Hands-on Experience & Tool Mastery

  • **Real-World Engagements:** Seek opportunities for internships, entry-level roles, or volunteer work to gain practical experience.
  • **Master Tools:** Go beyond basic familiarity with auditing tools. Become proficient with at least one GRC platform, a vulnerability scanner, and a SIEM system.
  • **Scripting & Automation:** Continue to hone your scripting skills to automate repetitive audit tasks and analyze large datasets more efficiently.

3. Networking & Community Engagement

  • **Join Professional Organizations:** Actively participate in organizations like ISACA, ISC², and local cybersecurity meetups.
  • **Mentorship:** Seek out experienced auditors who can guide your career path and provide valuable insights.
  • **Contribute to the Community:** Share your knowledge by writing articles, giving presentations, or contributing to open-source projects.

4. Develop Soft Skills

  • **Communication & Presentation:** Continue to refine your ability to communicate complex technical issues to both technical and non-technical audiences.
  • **Critical Thinking & Problem Solving:** These are paramount in auditing. Practice analyzing ambiguous situations and deriving sound conclusions.
  • **Negotiation & Persuasion:** Learn to effectively discuss findings and gain buy-in for recommendations.
  • **Time Management & Organization:** Crucial for managing multiple audit engagements and deadlines.
